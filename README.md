# BookStore_Team4

> E-commerce Website Project Planning for a Traditional Bookstore — Team 4.

BookStore_Team4 is a project planning initiative to help a traditional bookstore expand its business online, reach more customers, and improve operational efficiency.

**The primary deliverable is a complete project management documentation package ready for handover to a hypothetical development team. Building a fully functional website is not required by the assignment.** The features and technologies below describe the intended implementation scope, rather than completed functionality.

## 1. Background

The bookstore currently operates exclusively through a physical store. This creates several challenges:

- Customer reach is limited by location and opening hours.
- Business expansion and competitiveness are constrained in an increasingly digital market.
- Inventory management is inefficient.
- Limited customer data makes it difficult to analyze purchasing behavior and support business decisions.

The proposed e-commerce platform would allow customers to discover and purchase books online while supporting book, customer, order, and sales reporting operations.

## 2. Project Objectives

Team 4 acts as a **Project Management Board**, responsible for preparing an implementation plan that:

- Defines business needs, project objectives, and system scope.
- Documents functional requirements and proposed acceptance criteria.
- Breaks down work, establishes a schedule, and assigns responsibilities.
- Estimates resources and costs and identifies project risks.
- Defines quality, communication, and change management processes.
- Provides a clear documentation package for a hypothetical development team.

Expected business benefits include broader market reach, increased revenue, improved inventory management, and better insight into customer demand. These are intended outcomes, not measured results.

## 3. Planned Functional Scope

| Feature Area | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Book Management | Add, edit, and delete book information; manage categories and authors. |
| Shopping Cart and Checkout | Add books to the cart, update cart contents, and complete a simulated checkout. |
| Order Management | View orders and update order statuses. |
| Customer Management | Register, sign in, and manage personal information. |
| Search and Filtering | Search by book title, author, or genre; filter by price range. |
| Reports and Statistics | Generate basic revenue and bestselling-book reports. |
| System Administration | Manage role-based access for administrators and customers. |

### User Roles

| Role | Planned Responsibilities and Access |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Register, sign in, manage personal information, search for books, manage a cart, and complete simulated checkout. |
| Administrator | Manage books, categories, authors, and orders; view reports and administer access according to the approved design. |

### Scope Boundaries

- Payments are simulated; real financial transactions are outside the defined scope.
- Reporting is limited to basic revenue and bestselling-book statistics.
- A production-ready commercial website is not a required assignment deliverable.
- Additional features require an assessment of their impact on schedule and resources before approval.

## 5. Proposed Project Management Deliverables

The following documentation structure is proposed to support the assignment objectives. This list does not imply that these documents have already been completed or added to the repository.

| Deliverable | Main Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Project Charter | Background, objectives, stakeholders, high-level scope, and success criteria. |
| Requirements and Scope Document | Functional and non-functional requirements, scope boundaries, and acceptance criteria. |
| Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) | Work packages and their associated deliverables. |
| Project Schedule | Task sequence, dependencies, duration estimates, and milestones. |
| Resource and Responsibility Plan | Roles, responsibilities, and staffing requirements. |
| Cost Management Plan | Estimation assumptions, proposed budget, and contingency provisions. |
| Risk Management Plan | Risks, likelihood, impact, and response strategies. |
| Quality and Testing Plan | Quality standards, verification methods, and acceptance conditions. |
| Communication and Change Management Plan | Progress reporting, team coordination, and change request handling. |
| Handover Package | Approved documents, assumptions, open issues, and guidance for the development team. |

## 6. Proposed Planning Roadmap

1. **Initiation:** Clarify the business problem, objectives, and stakeholders.
2. **Requirements Analysis:** Define features, user roles, and scope boundaries.
3. **Planning:** Prepare the WBS, schedule, resource allocation, and estimated budget.
4. **Project Controls:** Establish risk, quality, communication, and change management plans.
5. **Review and Handover:** Check consistency, finalize the documentation, and prepare the handover package.

Specific dates, task owners, and budget figures will be established in the team's formal project plan.

## 7. Proposed Completion Criteria

- All seven feature areas in the assignment are covered.
- Requirements are clear, verifiable, and linked to acceptance criteria.
- Work packages, schedule, resources, and cost estimates are consistent.
- Major risks have assigned owners and response plans.
- Assumptions, constraints, and unresolved decisions are documented.
- The documentation is sufficiently detailed for a hypothetical development team to understand and begin implementation.
